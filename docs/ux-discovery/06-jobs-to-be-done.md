# Jobs-to-Be-Done Model (Draft)

**Workstream:** UX Discovery — Phase C/E  
**Date:** 2026-07-25  
**Evidence grade:** Stakeholder · Domain · Designer inference — **not yet validated with direct users**

Job statements will be revised after interviews. Do not treat as final.

---

## Core job (hypothesis)

When a high-stakes solicitation is in play, proposal teams want to **prove what the source requires, prevent missing submission obligations, and surface draft risks with evidence**, so they can **make an informed human readiness decision without false confidence**.

---

## Functional jobs

| Job ID | Circumstance            | Desired progress                                      | Current alternatives           | Barriers                            | Anxieties                              | Success                                          |
| ------ | ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| J-F1   | New RFP package arrives | Understand obligations fast with provenance           | PDF markup, spreadsheet matrix | Volume; addenda; tables             | Missing a mandatory item               | Can explain each critical obligation with source |
| J-F2   | Building response plan  | Stable list of forms, deadlines, owners               | Spreadsheet + email            | Stale versions; unclear ownership   | Someone thought “someone else owns it” | Blockers and owners visible                      |
| J-F3   | Draft nearing review    | Know unsupported / contradicted / proof-needed claims | Manual read-through            | Fatigue; version churn              | Shipping a false claim                 | Findings actionable with evidence                |
| J-F4   | Leadership checkpoint   | Explain readiness and residual risk                   | Slide decks, verbal            | Rebuilding narrative from fragments | Looking unprepared / overconfident     | One coherent, evidence-backed story              |
| J-F5   | After addendum          | Know what changed and what work is invalid            | Manual diff                    | Silent drift                        | Working to obsolete instructions       | Changed items and downstream impact clear        |
| J-F6   | Audit / protest defense | Reconstruct why a conclusion was reached              | Email archaeology              | Missing provenance                  | Cannot defend decision                 | Append-only trail with quotes                    |

## Emotional jobs

| Job ID | Progress sought                                                       |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| J-E1   | Feel calm under deadline pressure — uncertainty is named, not ambient |
| J-E2   | Feel justified escalating or blocking — evidence supports the call    |
| J-E3   | Avoid dread of “we missed something obvious”                          |

## Social jobs

| Job ID | Progress sought                                                                  |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| J-S1   | Show leadership a disciplined process (not heroics)                              |
| J-S2   | Hand work to SMEs without looking disorganized                                   |
| J-S3   | Demonstrate human control over AI assistance (credibility with legal/compliance) |

## Switching concerns (why teams might not adopt)

- Spreadsheet flexibility for custom columns
- Fear AI invents requirements
- Extra tool in an already fragmented stack
- Learning cost of multi-axis statuses
- Unclear whether it replaces or complements the matrix

## Design implication (hypothesis only)

Optimize for **J-F1, J-F2, J-F3, J-F4, J-F5** inside the product; treat drafting and bid/no-bid as adjacent jobs the UX should not pretend to absorb.
